Quick Emergency Travel Guidelines
Temple Crowd Safety
During Chithirai or Brahmotsavam festivals, agree on an outdoor landmark meeting point with your family before entering the temple corridors.
Heatstroke & Hydration
Carry Tender Coconut water (Elaneer) and electrolyte sachets during coastal summer pilgrimage visits to Madurai, Trichy, and Rameshwaram.
Rip Currents at Coast
Never swim alone along Marina, Kovalam, or Dhanushkodi beaches where warning flags are active. In an emergency, swim parallel to the shore.
