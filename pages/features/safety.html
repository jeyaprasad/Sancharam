Sacred Temple Etiquette
  • Traditional attire: Dhoti/Veshti for men, Sarees/Salwar for women.
  • Strictly avoid shorts, mini skirts, or sleeveless tops.
  • Deposit footwear at official free counter before Gopuram entry.
  • Mobile photography prohibited inside Garbhagriha sanctum.
Beach & Ocean Safety
  • Respect red flags: Marina & Dhanushkodi have violent undercurrents.
  • Swim only in designated zones: Kovalam Blue Flag & Mahabalipuram.
  • Stay with family groups during high tide (usually post 5 PM).
  • Coastal lifeguard towers are active daily from 6 AM to 6 PM.
Ghat & Mountain Driving
  • Sound horn gently before every hairpin bend blind curve.
  • Uphill vehicles always have right of way on single-lane passes.
  • Use second/low engine gear downhill; never ride continuous brakes.
  • Forest checkposts close at 6 PM (Mudumalai, Valparai, Kodaikanal).
