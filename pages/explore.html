Must-Visit Destinations

Historical

San Thome Basilica

Santhome Church in Chennai is one of the oldest churches in India. The Basilica sits over the tomb built over the mortal remains of one of the twelve Apostles of Jesus Christ.

Victoria Public Hall

Victoria Public Hall is a historical landmark built in honor of Queen Victoria’s Golden Jubilee, showcasing magnificent Indo-Saracenic architecure in Chennai.

Thanjavur Periya Kovil

The Thanjavur Periya Kovil, officially known as the Brihadeeswarar Temple, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and a global masterpiece of ancient Dravidian architecture.
