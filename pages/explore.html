UNESCO Heritage

Shore Temple Mahabalipuram

Bay of Bengal Coast, Chengalpattu District

Carved from monolithic granite blocks in 700–728 AD by Pallava King Narasimhavarman II. Stands as a timeless testament to Dravidian structural stone masonry over the sea.

Best: Year-Round

Rameswaram Island, Ramanathapuram District

One of India's twelve sacred Jyotirlingas, revered for its soaring yellow gopuram overlooking the Agni Theertham sea, 22 holy wells, and the world's longest corridor with 1,212 carved granite pillars.
